# Arc's own menus in place of Kodi's

The owner noticed some menus opened Kodi's own context menu rather than Arc's. Everywhere the
add-on used Kodi's `contextmenu`, `select` or `yesno` dialogs, it now uses one Arc menu, the same
panel and rows as the player's menus.

## What changed

- **`kodi/windows/menu.py` and `script-arc-menu.xml`:** a modal dialog centred over a dimmed
  screen, sized to its rows. It has a title, an optional message, the rows (white when focused,
  with a tick on the current choice), and Back or Context Menu to close. `choose()` picks from a
  list; `confirm()` asks a question. The rows' layouts are now one shared include
  (`MenuLayouts`), used by the player's menus and this dialog.
- **The card menu**, as the Apple app's `TitleActions` and the Android card menu. The choice of
  actions is in `arc/menus.py` (tested):
  - Play, or Resume and Play from the Beginning;
  - Mark Watched or Mark Unwatched;
  - Add to or Remove from Watchlist (films and shows);
  - Go to Show (an episode, except on its show's page);
  - Remove from Continue Watching (on that row only).
- **Where the menu opens:** it was only on Home before. It now opens on:
  - Home;
  - the library, collection, playlist and person grids;
  - search results;
  - the title page: its episodes, Related, and the title itself (Watched and Watchlist).

  Play on a card in a grid, search or Related plays it, as on Home.
- **The change shows at once:**
  - Home reloads its rows;
  - a grid card updates its tick and progress;
  - a show's page reloads its episodes.

  If Arc can't save a change, a short notice says so.
- **Home keeps the focus when a row refreshes.** Kodi drops the focus from a list while it's
  emptied and refilled. The focus now goes back to the row, or to the nearest row with cards if
  the last card was removed.
- **Sort, Filter, the playlist picker and the sign-out question** use the Arc menu. Sign Out now
  asks "Sign out of <server>?".
- **Sort and Filter pills:** Down from them now reaches the grid; they had no Up/Down/Left/Right
  set before.

Kodi's keyboard (search, the server address) and number pad (PINs) remain: an on-screen keyboard
and a PIN pad are on the feature-parity list (search; settings and sign-in). Kodi's small
notifications are kept for errors.

## Checked

- `python3 -m pytest -q`: 53 passed (new `tests/test_menus.py`).
- `python3 tools/check.py`: all good.
- Kodi 20 on a virtual display against a scratch Arc:
  - Home's menu on Continue Watching: Remove worked, and the focus moved to the next row when the
    row emptied;
  - an episode's menu showed Go to Show;
  - Sort, with the current choice ticked;
  - Mark Unwatched in the Films grid cleared the card's tick;
  - the sign-out question;
  - the title page's own menu and the menu on a Related card.

## Logging

A failed card action logs a warning naming the action and the error type. A saved one logs at
debug.
