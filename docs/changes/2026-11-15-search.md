# Search as you type

Item 5 of the feature-parity list, first half (`docs/plans/feature-parity.md`). Search was Kodi's
keyboard dialog, then one grid of films, shows and episodes. It's now the Apple app's search, with
Arc's own on-screen keyboard.

## What changed

- **The keyboard**: Arc's own keys at the left (a-z and 1-0, six across, then Space, Delete and
  Clear), white when focused. A remote's number keys type digits too.
- **The search box** above the keys is a Kodi text field. While it has focus (Up from the keys),
  a PC keyboard types straight into it, OK opens Kodi's keyboard, and phone remote apps (Kore,
  Yatse) type into it. The keys and the box show the same text.
  - Why a text field: Kodi runs its own shortcuts for some letters before a window hears them
    (S opens the power menu, E the TV guide), so a window can't catch typing itself. A focused
    text field gets every key, and Kodi reports each change as a click on it.
- **As you type**: Arc is asked once typing pauses for 0.35 s, as the Apple app does; an older
  answer arriving late is ignored.
- **Rows**, in the Apple app's order, each shown only when it has matches: Movies, TV Shows,
  Episodes (wide cards), Collections, People (round photos and what they're known for) and
  Genres (a card per library: "Drama · Films").
  - Titles open their page (an episode opens its show on its season); Play plays and Context
    Menu gives the card's actions, as elsewhere.
  - A person opens their page, a collection its page.
  - A genre opens its library's Browse tab filtered to it (`LibraryWindow(genre=...)`).
- Back from the rows returns to the keys; Back from the keys closes search.
- No matches says "No matches for “…”."; a failed search says Arc couldn't be reached.
- The words and rows are `arc/search.py` (tested in `tests/test_search.py`).
- The focused-row second-line grey is now a colour constant (`on-white-muted`) in
  `skin/includes.xml`, shared by the menu and the genre cards.

## Checked

- `python3 -m pytest -q`: 79 passed. `python3 tools/check.py`: all good.
- Kodi 20 on a virtual display against a scratch Arc (not real hardware):
  - typed on the PC keyboard into the box ("sere" found Firefly's "Serenity"; S no longer
    opens the power menu);
  - typed with the remote on the keys, and Delete;
  - text sent as a phone remote does (`Input.SendText`) searched for "blade": Movies and the
    Blade Runner collection;
  - "dra": People (Larry Drake, opening his page) and Genres (Drama in Films and in TV Shows;
    the TV one opened TV Shows' Browse filtered to Drama, showing Firefly);
  - Back from each page returned to search with the rows as they were.

## Logging

- "search failed: <error type>" at warning; "search: <n> rows" at debug. Never what was typed.

## Limits

- The keys are English letters and digits; accented letters come from a keyboard or Kodi's
  keyboard (Arc ignores accents anyway).
- Recent searches aren't kept (the Apple app doesn't either).
