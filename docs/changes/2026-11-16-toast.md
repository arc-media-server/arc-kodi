# Arc's own toast

Short messages used Kodi's notification, drawn by the user's skin. The owner asked for Arc's own.

## What changed

- A toast at the top right of whichever Arc window is showing: a dark rounded panel, Arc's TV
  mark, and the message in white (two lines at most). It slides in, stays four seconds and fades.
  A newer message replaces it; it never takes the focus, so the remote keeps working.
- Drawn by a shared skin piece (`ArcToast` in `skin/includes.xml`), in every window but the
  player (which has its own notice), from a global property (`arc.toast` on Kodi's Home window)
  that `kodi/toast.py` sets and clears. Its timers stop when the add-on closes, so Kodi isn't
  kept waiting.
- Every Kodi notification now uses it:
  - Arc couldn't be reached (sign-in, the profile list, a title page that didn't load);
  - Arc's own sign-in and start-up errors (its message);
  - Play All / Shuffle couldn't start; a ⋯ change wasn't saved; no playlists ("Nothing here
    yet."); a title couldn't be played;
  - Watch Together notices while the player isn't open (in the player they already used its
    notice).

The keyboard (#22) and invite (#21) windows are dialogs over a window: the toast shows on the
window underneath; adding the piece to them can follow once they're merged.

## Logging

Nothing new: the call sites already log their failures. The messages aren't logged again.

## Checked

Kodi on Xvfb against a scratch Arc: Playlists with none showed "Nothing here yet." at the top
right, Down still moved along the sidebar while it showed, and it cleared after four seconds.
A two-line message wasn't seen on screen. Not on real hardware.
