# Kodi batch 5: owner's fixes from the Ugoos

1. **Player Info:** the panel is a white shape tinted with Arc's `surface` colour, so a skin's
   colour (blue on the owner's) never shows through. Passthrough audio reads "pt-eac3 ·
   Passthrough", not Kodi's "RAW, RAW, …". Deinterlacing is left out for progressive video.
2. **Sort names** as the web's and Android's: Recently Added, Recently Watched, Length.
3. **Quality badges** as Android's capsules: SDR when there's no HDR, and the codec with its
   channels together ("AAC 2.0", "DD+ 5.1"), then Atmos. Play Version… uses the same words.
4. **Starring / Directed by:** one line each, left-aligned, cut short if long, as Android's.
5. **Sidebar:** the profile's picture (its colour and initial) at the top; choosing it switches
   profile, as Android's and LG/Samsung's.
6. **Settings:** "Show Missing Episodes" in Title Case. The TMDB/TheTVDB credit leaves the space
   under the picture and opens from the About row.
7. **Profiles:** no server name under "Who's watching?".
8. **Search:** movies and shows together as **Top Results**, alternating, as Android's (Kodi's
   letter grid stays).

9. **Home:** the first row sits at the foot of the screen, as Android's: a thumb or backdrop
   row (Continue Watching) has a 500-high slot instead of 620, and the hero takes the rest above
   it (`arc/home.py` `hero_height`), its text moving down with it. Checked at 1920x1080: the
   captions end about 40 px above the bottom edge.
10. **Kodi's Player Info:** a second press of O or Info while Arc's panel shows, or its new
    "Kodi's Player Info" button, closes Arc's panel and opens Kodi's own Player Process Info
    (CoreELEC's shows more, such as Dolby Vision FEL/MEL). Arc's player stops closing that
    dialog while it was asked for, and hides its own controls; Back, Info or O closes it and
    returns to playback.
